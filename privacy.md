### 1. Overview
aswebuild.com (the "Website"), operated by LinQreative (including its owner and operator, "we", "us", or "our"), respects your privacy and handles personal information in accordance with the Personal Information Protection Act (British Columbia) ("PIPA") and applicable Canadian federal laws.  

### 2. Information We Collect
* **Information You Provide Voluntarily**: When you subscribe to our newsletter, submit a form, or contact us by email, we collect your email address and any information you choose to include in your message.  

* **Automated Technical Data**: When you visit the Website, basic technical data (such as IP address, browser type, device type, and pages visited) may be collected automatically through cookies or analytics tools to help us understand Website traffic and improve our content.  

### 3. How We Use Your Information
We use your information solely to send you newsletters or updates you have subscribed to, respond to your inquiries, maintain Website security, and analyze aggregate readership trends. Under Canada's Anti-Spam Legislation (CASL), you may unsubscribe from our email communications at any time using the unsubscribe link in any email or by contacting us directly.  

### 4. Third-Party Services and Affiliate Links
We use reputable third-party service providers (such as GitHub Pages for hosting, Formspark or Substack for email forms and delivery, and analytics tools) to operate the Website. In addition, when you click an affiliate link on the Website, the third-party merchant or affiliate network may place a tracking cookie on your browser to attribute referrals. We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties.  

### 5. Data Retention and Your Rights
We retain personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this Policy or as required by law. You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information at any time by contacting our Privacy Officer at [hello@aswebuild.com](mailto:hello@aswebuild.com).
